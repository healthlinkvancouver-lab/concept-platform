The Concept Platform

Business concepts, sold by being used.

Every other marketplace asks a buyer to leap on documents and promises. This one removes the leap: the buyer operates the concept — in simulation or as a real pilot inside their own business — before buying it, and every pilot is a conditional purchase signed on day one: fixed scope, fixed end date, agreed metrics. Hit the metrics and the sale completes at the asking price. Miss them, and nobody is trapped.

How a purchase works here

1. Simulated demoThe buyer runs the concept's economics — its numbers, not screenshots — and reaches a real outcome.
2. Data roomDisclosure opens in stages as the buyer deepens, so a seller can be verified without being copied.
3. Paid pilotThe concept runs live inside the buyer's own business, under the conditional purchase signed on day one.
4. Escrowed closeIf the agreed metrics are met by the agreed date, the purchase completes at the asking price — and the pilot instance becomes the purchased business. Nothing resets.

#003

Safe-Pod

Design$35,000 CAD

A flat-packed, 100% biodegradable wax-cardboard emergency shelter (~$15 a unit) that deploys in minutes and breaks down within about 48 hours — so a support worker has to check back in. The shelter creates the contact; the contact carries the care. Design complete; an outdoor pilot is approved in principle by Dr. Conway — a potential Vancouver first. The pilot has not yet been run.

#004

GVRU — Renters Union

Design$25,000 CAD

A renters union built as a membership organization: $15–20 a month in dues, a pledge-card campaign that builds committed membership before launch, and benefits no individual renter can get alone — rent coverage during illness, appliance repair. Two research papers included. (Its rent-coverage benefit may fall under BC's Financial Institutions Act; no public benefit announcement before a legal opinion — the regulatory path passes to the buyer.)

#005

Willing

Design$20,000 CAD

A self-help app for huge life changes, built on willingness, not fixing — people are not broken; a problem truly seen arrives with its solution. Change is walked through with a trusted Guide whose voice carries the encouragement, an AI Doctor that maps which life events shaped which habits, and a Circle of supporters. Full Guide / Doctor / Circle design included. Concept and design only — nothing built.

#006

Project SAFE

Design$15,000 CAD

A jewelry wearable for vulnerable people working in isolated, high-risk settings: scheduled check-ins where a missed confirmation is the alarm — no button press required from someone who may no longer be able to act. Graduated escalation through trusted contacts, privacy by design, and a chronological record for investigators. Concept only — nothing built.

#007
